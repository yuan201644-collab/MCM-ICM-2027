SUMMARY: The Great Lakes serve as the heart of North America with their ebbs and flows providing the lifeblood of both the surrounding environment and societies. Because of the lake’s incredible importance, it is in the best interest of the world to protect them. Plan 2014, proposed by the International Joint Committee (ĲC), manages the water in the Great Lakes by building up water behind both the Compensating Works dam at the Sault St. Marie River and Moses-Saunders dam and taking action as needed. To better serve the Great Lakes region, we seek to expand Plan 2014 to regulate water levels optimizing for human needs and the preservation of ecosystems. To determine optimal dam scheduling we modeled the Great Lakes as a dynamic flow network and created two control algorithms that utilize linear programming to solve for optimal dam schedules and water levels.
