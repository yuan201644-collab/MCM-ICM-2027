SUMMARY: After losing contact with the main ship, a suitable positioning model, search equipment, and search strategy for the submersible can signiﬁcantly reduce rescue time and increase the success rate of the rescue mission. In this paper, based on diﬀerent malfunction scenarios of the sub- mersible, a positioning model and search strategy for the submersible’s movement have been es- tablished, providing some suggestion for maritime rescue. For Problem 1, ﬁrstly, we searched for the depth and ocean current information in the Ionian Sea and ﬁtted the seaﬂoor topography. Then, a location model is conducted. Utilizing the fourth- order Runge-Kutta method, numerical solutions are obtained, and potential motion trajectories are plotted.
